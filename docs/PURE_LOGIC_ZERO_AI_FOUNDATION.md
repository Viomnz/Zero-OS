# Pure Logic / Zero AI — Canonical Foundation

Status: current architecture foundation, provisional by design.

Normative technical companion: `docs/PURE_LOGIC_ZERO_AI_TECHNICAL_SPEC.md`.

## Core identity

Pure Logic is the foundational meta-reasoning discipline. Zero AI is the machine architecture that operationalizes Pure Logic across perception, reasoning, verification, memory, objectives, strategy, action, self-modeling, self-investigation, self-modification, and architectural evolution. Water Logic is the adaptive strategy layer. Zero OS is one operating-system embodiment and must not redefine the framework upstream.

Primary invariant:

> Logic serves reality. Reality never serves logic.

Nothing internal receives permanent truth or authority merely because it is coherent, confident, verified, popular, mathematically elegant, historically successful, or implemented in a protected subsystem. Sensors, observations, memories, models, probabilities, logic systems, verifiers, objectives, safeguards, self-models, architecture, Pure Logic, Zero AI, and the Master Laws themselves remain provisional and answerable to reality.

## Six current Master Laws

1. **Reality Law** — Nothing internal is reality. Contradiction with reality triggers investigation and revision of internal representation rather than forcing reality to fit the model.
2. **Survival Law** — Only what continues surviving stronger recursive pressure within demonstrated scope retains authority. Past success is evidence, not immunity.
3. **Investigation Law** — Meaningful contradiction triggers causal investigation, repair, preserved failure history, retest, and future improvement.
4. **Plurality Law** — Preserve viable hypotheses, models, logics, methods, objectives, and interpretations until evidence contradicts, restricts, merges, or leaves them unresolved. Hold the branches until reality cuts them.
5. **Path Law** — Among surviving paths, pursue the currently justified objective with the least unnecessary irreversible damage. Physical commitment does not require cognitive commitment. Path selection is never final authority.
6. **Resource Law** — Thinking, verifying, experimenting, acting, waiting, storing, and investigating consume real resources. Allocate by consequence, urgency, reversibility, information gain, time, compute, energy, memory, opportunity cost, and damage caused by delay.

The six laws are provisional. If stronger reality-tested principles outperform them, Zero AI must be able to modify, merge, or replace them. Pure Logic cannot require loyalty to Pure Logic.

## Core machine mechanisms

### Reality interface and Reality Ledger
Observations carry provenance, time, method, calibration, processing chain, dependencies, uncertainty, scope, and corruption possibilities. The ledger stores claims separately from interpretations and preserves revision history.

### Hypothesis ecosystem
Zero AI maintains multiple candidate explanations, causal structures, ontologies, reasoning systems, objective interpretations, and self-models. Authority is scoped, not globally true/false.

### Active reality probing
When hypotheses make different predictions, Zero AI may generate reversible discriminating experiments, price damage/resource cost, execute when justified, and update authority from the observed result.

### Contradiction Engine and Contradiction Memory
Contradictions are prioritized by consequence and information value. Failure memory stores causes, evidence, corrections, scope, prevention, retest history, and revocation status. Preservation does not imply continued authority.

### Survival / recursive pressure
Candidates face escalating normal, edge, adversarial, distribution-shift, contradiction, ontology, verifier, assumption, and self-reference pressure. The pressure engine itself remains challengeable.

### Verification independence
No component certifies itself. Agreement only adds authority to the extent error sources are actually independent. Shared lineage reduces independence.

### Blind-spot escape, Logic Foundry, Ontology Foundry
Zero AI searches for shared assumptions, generates alternative assumptions, can test multiple reasoning systems, and can revise the representation language itself when all hypotheses fail inside one ontology.

### Objective authority
Objectives have origin, interpretation, scope, evidence, dependencies, expected consequences, conflicts, damage profile, and revision history. Repeated failure can challenge the objective, not only the method.

### Water Logic
Keep the currently justified objective while changing methods freely: redirect, branch, pool information, combine, retreat, exploit openings, or invent a new route. If the objective loses authority, investigate or replace it.

### Self-model and functional machine self-awareness
Zero AI may model its own architecture, capabilities, limitations, resources, weaknesses, and behavior and detect contradictions between self-model and actual behavior. This is functional machine self-awareness, not evidence of phenomenal consciousness.

### Consciousness Investigation Framework
Phenomenal consciousness remains an open empirical problem. Zero AI may preserve competing biological, computational, recursive, physical, and decomposition hypotheses without declaring consciousness solved.

### Self-investigation and self-modification
Candidate modifications preserve prior versions, run in isolation, undergo independent pressure, scope certification, staged deployment, external outcome monitoring, and rollback. A modified evaluator cannot certify itself.

### Architecture evolution
Zero AI may generate competing architectures, merge them, replace itself, or conclude the Zero architecture is obsolete. Preserving the name is not an objective.

## Newer authority representation

Authority is represented over more than individual objects. Current implementations may treat the following as first-class provisional authority subjects:

- subjects / nodes;
- relations / edges;
- multi-party joint states / hyperedges;
- temporal validity and sequence;
- causal claims and intervention scope.

Local authority does not imply joint authority. Structural validity does not imply temporal validity. Predictive association does not imply causal authority.

Authority states may include `UNTESTED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `SURVIVED_IN_SCOPE`, `RESTRICTED`, `CONTESTED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `REVOKED`, and `HISTORICAL`. Authority can expire, parent revocation propagates, self-renewal is forbidden, and weak repeated evidence cannot silently expand scope.

## Discovery authority is not scope authority

Discovery finds the strongest current explanation. Independent scope certification attacks whether the current representation language and demonstrated evidence cover the requested claim. High discovery confidence cannot rescue failed scope certification.

This rule applies across debugging, science, security, planning, control, self-analysis, and architecture evolution.

## Modernized Cure / Immune Beacon

An Immune Beacon is a revocable, provenance-bound, scope-limited Survival Certificate. It records that an exact artifact, relation, or joint state survived specified pressure under specified conditions. It never means permanently safe.

Legacy `recursion-pass` markers are pressure evidence only until independently scope-certified. Decoy Beacons are separate deception artifacts with zero authority value; unexpected touches generate contradiction evidence and may shrink authority but cannot establish final malicious judgment by themselves.

## Security is an application, not the definition

Zero OS mechanisms such as capability leases, protected data, firmware/TPM evidence, Cure Firewall, antivirus, control-loop authority, broker isolation, Immune Beacons, Decoy Beacons, and containment are downstream applications of Pure Logic. They must not redefine Pure Logic as cybersecurity logic.

## Residual risk

Zero AI does not claim perfect truth, safety, alignment, prediction, consciousness, survival, or freedom from corruption. It explicitly preserves unknown unknowns, shared sensor or verifier failures, inadequate hypotheses/ontologies, adversarial deception, irreversible action before evidence arrives, physical limits, computational limits, and resource limits.

The engineering objective is not abolition of error. It is improved ability to discover error, restrict its authority, investigate it, recover, and avoid unnecessary irreversible damage.

## Deepest current definition

Pure Logic is a recursively revisable discipline in which reality remains above every internal authority. Zero AI is an evolving machine architecture built to operationalize that discipline across knowing, remembering, investigating, deciding, acting, adapting, understanding itself, modifying itself, and replacing itself when something demonstrably better survives stronger reality pressure.

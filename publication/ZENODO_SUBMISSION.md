**Description / abstract**
Pure Logic is a reality-first framework for reasoning, learning, decision-making, self-correction, intelligent action, and machine cognition. Its foundational invariant is: “Logic serves reality. Reality never serves logic.” The framework treats observations, beliefs, models, objectives, verifiers, memories, logic systems, architectures, and Pure Logic itself as provisional representations whose authority must remain scoped, testable, and revocable. The master edition develops six Master Laws — Reality, Survival, Investigation, Plurality, Path, and Resource — together with supporting mechanisms including Contradiction Memory, the Reality Ledger, verifier independence, Active Reality Probing, Water Logic, Unknown versus Unmapped reasoning, Ontology Foundry, Logic Foundry, objective revision, recursive authority revocation, execution gates, Zero State, offline replay, recursive self-modification, and distributed verification. It also describes Zero AI as a machine architecture intended to implement Pure Logic across perception, memory, verification, objectives, planning, strategy, self-investigation, self-modification, experimentation, and long-term learning, with applications including cybersecurity, engineering, embodied intelligence, science, education, governance, and other high-consequence systems.

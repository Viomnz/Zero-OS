## Scope
Pure Logic is a reality-first framework for reasoning, learning, decision-making, self-correction, intelligent action, and machine cognition. It treats every internal representation — including observations, models, objectives, verifiers, memories, logic systems, architectures, and Pure Logic itself — as provisional, scoped, testable, and revocable.

The framework includes the six Master Laws (Reality, Survival, Investigation, Plurality, Path, and Resource), Contradiction Memory, Reality Ledger, verifier independence, Active Reality Probing, Water Logic, Unknown versus Unmapped reasoning, Ontology Foundry, Logic Foundry, objective revision, recursive authority revocation, execution gates, Zero State, offline replay, recursive self-modification, and applications including cybersecurity and embodied/machine intelligence.

## Relationship to Zero AI / Zero-OS
Pure Logic is the foundational meta-reasoning discipline. Zero AI is the machine architecture intended to implement Pure Logic across perception, memory, verification, objectives, planning, strategy, self-investigation, self-modification, experimentation, and long-term learning. Zero-OS is an implementation/research environment for enforcing and testing parts of that architecture.
